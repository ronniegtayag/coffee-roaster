# Schema gap analysis: business flow vs `prisma/schema.prisma`

Owner: product-owner. This file describes **needs**, not designs. The `architect` decides the models and fields.
Reference flow: `docs/domain/wholesale-roaster-flow.md`. Open questions: `docs/domain/questions-for-roaster.md`.

## Severity

- **Must**: the MVP can't support a normal wholesale week without it, or it is a compliance risk.
- **Should**: needed soon after MVP. Without it, the business keeps working in spreadsheets or other tools.
- **Later**: valuable, but only once the basics are in use.

Several items depend on one answer: **does the accounting system own invoices and payments?** Those rows are marked "(depends on Q1)".

**Update 2026-11-19:** the roaster uses **MYOB**, not Xero. Which MYOB product and who creates invoices are still open (Q1a to Q1c). The working recommendation is in `myob-integration.md`: our app prepares invoices and creates them in MYOB, and MYOB owns numbering, payments, credits and BAS. Severities below assume that recommendation. Where a row says "Xero", read "MYOB".

## 1. What the schema has today (summary)

| Area       | Models                                                                                                                             | Notes                                                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customers  | `Customer` (billed entity, `abn`, `paymentTermsDays`), `Branch` (delivery location)                                                | A good fit for cafes with several venues.                             |
| Catalogue  | `Product` (one SKU: `origin`, `roastLevel`, `weightGrams`, one `unitPriceExGst`, `taxCode`)                                        | One price for everyone. Coffee and pack size are combined in one row. |
| Stock      | `InventoryItem` (`quantityOnHand` units per product, `reorderLevel`)                                                               | Roasted, packed units only. There is no history.                      |
| Orders     | `Order` (per `Branch`, `status`, `requestedDeliveryDate`, optional `invoiceId`), `OrderItem` (qty, snapshotted price and tax code) | Ad-hoc orders only.                                                   |
| Invoicing  | `Invoice` (header totals only), `Payment` (one invoice each)                                                                       | No invoice lines and no credit notes.                                 |
| Purchasing | `Supplier`, `SupplierInvoice` (header totals only)                                                                                 | Not linked to anything bought.                                        |

## 2. Gaps

| #   | Capability                                                                                             | Why the business needs it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Schema today                                                                                                                                     | Severity                                                                                                                     |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G1  | **Coffee vs packaged SKU split** (one coffee, many bag sizes and grinds)                               | Roast planning works in **kg of a coffee**, not in bags. "House Blend" is sold as 1 kg whole bean, 250 g retail, and maybe 1 kg espresso-ground. These must add up to one roast demand.                                                                                                                                        | `Product` combines coffee + pack in one row. `origin`/`roastLevel` sit on the SKU. There is no grind field and no parent "coffee".               | **Must**                                                                                                                     |
| G2  | **Per-customer pricing / price lists**                                                                 | Wholesale prices vary per account (volume, equipment on loan, negotiated deals), typically $22–$42+/kg. Order entry must pick the right price automatically.                                                                                                                                                                   | Only `Product.unitPriceExGst`. `OrderItem.unitPriceExGst` snapshots the price but has nowhere to get a customer price from.                      | **Must**                                                                                                                     |
| G3  | **Standing (recurring) orders**                                                                        | Most wholesale volume repeats weekly on fixed days, per branch. Staff need to generate the week's orders, skip or override one occurrence, and pause an account.                                                                                                                                                               | None. Only one-off `Order`.                                                                                                                      | **Must**                                                                                                                     |
| G4  | **Roast/delivery calendar and cut-offs**                                                               | An order belongs to a roast day and a delivery day. Late orders roll to the next slot. Public holidays move days.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Only `Order.requestedDeliveryDate`. There is no roast date, delivery day pattern per branch, or cut-off.                                         | **Must** (simple form: delivery days per branch + roast date per order)                                                      |
| G5  | **Production plan: kg to roast per coffee per roast day**                                              | The daily question in a roastery. It needs G1 (coffee-level kg), orders by date, and expected yield.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Can't be derived. There is no coffee-level grouping and no yield.                                                                                | **Must** (as a report/query; batches themselves are G7)                                                                      |
| G6  | **Invoice lines snapshotted at issue + tax-invoice fields**                                            | An ATO tax invoice must show each item's description, quantity, price and GST status, and must be kept unchanged for 5 years. Today the lines are read live from `OrderItem`, which can still be edited. Freight, credits and misc charges have no order item to live on. The seller's ABN/identity must print on the invoice. | `Invoice` has totals only. Lines are implied through `Order.invoiceId` → `OrderItem`. No seller details. Nothing stops edits after `ISSUED`.     | **Must**. MYOB issues the invoice (recommended), so the CRM keeps a frozen "what was billed" record plus the MYOB invoice ID |
| G7  | **Green bean inventory by lot**                                                                        | This is the main raw material. Needed for buying decisions ("do we have enough Brazil for next week?") and cost. Counted in kg per lot, received in bags.                                                                                                                                                                      | None. `InventoryItem` is roasted units per SKU only.                                                                                             | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G8  | **Roast batches with green-in / roasted-out weights**                                                  | Records actual roast loss (about 12–20%, varying by lot and roast), uses up green stock, and is the source of yield for planning and cost.                                                                                                                                                                                     | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G9  | **Blend recipes**                                                                                      | A blend is a set mix of component coffees that changes as lots run out. Green demand comes from recipe × kg needed.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Should** (Must if most volume is blends, see Q5)                                                                           |
| G10 | **Supplier invoices linked to green purchases and landed cost**                                        | Landed cost per kg = green price + freight + other charges, spread over the lot. It feeds COGS and margin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | `SupplierInvoice` has header totals only. There are no lines, no link to a lot or purchase, and no supplier type (importer, packaging, freight). | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G11 | **Traceability: lot → batch → packed bags → delivered order**                                          | A wholesale food business must have a written recall system (FSANZ Std 3.2.2 cl.12). Being able to answer "who received batch X" makes a recall targeted.                                                                                                                                                                      | None. There is no batch or roast date on stock or order lines.                                                                                   | **Should** (Later if the roaster keeps paper/Cropster records, see Q11)                                                      |
| G12 | **Credit notes / adjustment notes**                                                                    | Used for short or damaged deliveries, wrong coffee and price errors. Issued invoices can't be edited. For taxable lines an adjustment note is generally required for the GST decrease.                                                                                                                                         | None. `InvoiceStatus.VOID` is the only reversal.                                                                                                 | **Later** if MYOB owns credit notes (recommended, Q1c). The CRM only needs to show them.                                     |     |
| G13 | **Payments allocated across several invoices**                                                         | One bank transfer often pays several invoices. Part-payments happen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `Payment.invoiceId` is a single FK.                                                                                                              | **Later**. MYOB allocates payments. The CRM reads back `Status`/`BalanceDueAmount` per invoice.                              |     |
| G14 | **Credit control: credit limit, account on hold, overdue visibility at order time**                    | Supply stops when an account is overdue. The account manager must see this before dispatch.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Only `Customer.paymentTermsDays` and `isActive`. No hold flag, no credit limit, no overdue derivation surfaced.                                  | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G15 | **Payment terms beyond "N days"**                                                                      | Terms like "end of month + 30", "7 days", COD and prepaid are common.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `paymentTermsDays Int @default(14)` only. The default of 14 is itself an assumption.                                                             | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G16 | **Multiple contacts per customer/branch**                                                              | Owner, head barista (orders), and accounts (invoices, remittances) each need contact details and their own emails.                                                                                                                                                                                                             | One `billingEmail` on `Customer`. One `contactName`/`contactPhone` on `Branch`.                                                                  | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G17 | **Order fulfilment detail: partial delivery, backorder, substitution**                                 | If a lot runs out or a coffee is short, part of the order ships, the rest is backordered or substituted, and only what was delivered is invoiced.                                                                                                                                                                              | `OrderItem.quantity` only. There are no delivered/backordered quantities. One `Order.status` covers the whole order.                             | **Should** (Must if invoicing is on dispatch and shortages are frequent, see Q9)                                             |
| G18 | **Delivery / dispatch records**                                                                        | Courier vs own run, tracking number, delivery docket, proof of delivery, delivery fee.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Only `OrderStatus.DISPATCHED/DELIVERED` and `Branch.deliveryNotes`.                                                                              | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G19 | **Non-product charge lines (freight, minimum-order fee, service call)**                                | Delivery fees below a minimum and equipment servicing are billed, and their GST may differ from coffee.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Lines must reference a `Product`. There is no charge type. A dummy "Freight" product would work as a stopgap.                                    | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G20 | **Minimum order rules**                                                                                | Per order, per week, or monthly spend. Also tied to equipment-loan commitments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Later** (unless Q7 says they are enforced)                                                                                 |
| G21 | **Equipment on loan and servicing**                                                                    | Machines and grinders lent against a kg/week commitment. Needs asset register, serials, install date, service history, contract term.                                                                                                                                                                                          | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Later** (Should if the roaster runs a big loan program, see Q10)                                                           |
| G22 | **Accounting integration references (MYOB)**                                                           | MYOB is the ledger. Customers (MYOB cards), items, tax codes and invoices need MYOB IDs and sync status. Our `GST`/`GST_FREE` must map to the file's MYOB tax codes (e.g. `GST`, `FRE`). The OAuth connection needs a stored refresh token per company file.                                                                   | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Must** (MYOB confirmed; product/online file still to confirm, Q1a).                                                        |
| G23 | **Stock movement history (ledger)**                                                                    | Auditing stock changes (received, roasted, packed, dispatched, written off), plus stocktakes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `InventoryItem.quantityOnHand` is a bare number. There is no movement history.                                                                   | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G24 | **Packaging/consumables stock**                                                                        | Bags, valves and labels run out and stop packing. They also feed COGS.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | None.                                                                                                                                            | **Later**                                                                                                                    |
| G25 | **COGS / margin reporting**                                                                            | Margin per kg by customer and product. Needs G7, G8, G10 and the accountant's costing method.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Not derivable.                                                                                                                                   | **Later**                                                                                                                    |
| G26 | **Product attributes for labels** (process, tasting notes, roast-for espresso/filter, shelf life days) | Labels and price sheets. Best-before = roast date + shelf life.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `origin`, `roastLevel` only.                                                                                                                     | **Later**                                                                                                                    |
| G27 | **Audit trail (who did what)**                                                                         | Price overrides, credit notes and holds need accountability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `User` exists but no model records `createdBy`/`updatedBy`.                                                                                      | **Should**                                                                                                                   |
| G28 | **Customer self-service ordering / portal accounts**                                                   | Cafes ordering directly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `User` is staff only (`UserRole`).                                                                                                               | **Later**                                                                                                                    |

## 3. Things in the schema that look wrong or over-built for the real flow

| Item                                                                    | Concern                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Suggestion for architect to consider                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OrderStatus.ROASTING` / `PACKED` on `Order`                            | Roasting happens in **batches across many orders**, not per order. One order can include a coffee that is roasted and another that isn't. A single order-level production status will be wrong for half the order.    | Keep order status about the commercial and dispatch lifecycle. Derive production progress from batches/packing later. |
| `Invoice` has no lines; lines come from `Order.invoiceId` → `OrderItem` | Breaks tax-invoice immutability (G6). An `onDelete: SetNull` on `Order.invoice` means deleting an invoice silently orphans orders. Issued invoices should never be deleted, only voided or credited.                  | Frozen invoice lines. Restrict deletion of issued invoices.                                                           |
| Whole CRM invoicing + `Payment` + `SupplierInvoice` modules             | The roaster uses MYOB, so these duplicate the ledger: bill entry, payment reconciliation, aged debtors, BAS. Building them fully would be wasted effort.                                                              | The MVP should only _create_ invoices in MYOB and _read back_ paid status (`myob-integration.md`). Settle Q1b/Q1c.    |
| `Product.unitPriceExGst` as the only price                              | Works as a "list price" but not as the price customers actually pay (G2).                                                                                                                                             | Treat it as a default/list price.                                                                                     |
| `Product.origin` / `roastLevel`                                         | Blends have several origins. Roasters often describe roasts by brew method (espresso/filter) rather than light/dark. The `RoastLevel` enum may not match how this roaster talks.                                      | Move to the coffee level (G1). Confirm vocabulary (Q5).                                                               |
| `InventoryItem` with `onDelete: Cascade` from `Product`                 | Deleting a product silently deletes its stock record. Products should be deactivated (`isActive`), not deleted, once ordered. `OrderItem` already restricts this, so the cascade is inconsistent.                     | Restrict.                                                                                                             |
| `InventoryItem.quantityOnHand` (packed units) as _the_ inventory        | For a roast-to-order wholesaler, packed stock is small and short-lived. The valuable stock is **green**. The current model tracks the less important stock and misses the important one.                              | Re-prioritise per G7.                                                                                                 |
| `Customer.abn @unique`                                                  | Usually fine. It breaks if one legal entity (one ABN) is set up as two customer records (e.g. two separately billed brands under one company). ABN is also optional, so sole traders without an ABN on file are fine. | Confirm with roaster (Q4) whether one ABN can be billed separately per brand.                                         |
| `Customer.paymentTermsDays @default(14)`                                | The default terms are an unconfirmed guess. Can't express EOM terms (G15).                                                                                                                                            | Confirm (Q8).                                                                                                         |
| `Payment.paidAt` as `timestamptz`                                       | The bank settlement date is a business date (Sydney), like `issueDate`/`dueDate`.                                                                                                                                     | Consider a plain date, consistent with CLAUDE.md conventions.                                                         |
| `TaxCode` = `GST` / `GST_FREE` only                                     | Fine for sales today. Supplier bills (imports, any input-taxed items) and Xero mapping may need more codes later. Product classification itself is **confirm with accountant**.                                       | Keep for MVP. Map to MYOB tax codes.                                                                                  |
| `InvoiceStatus` has no `OVERDUE`                                        | Correct. Overdue should be derived from `dueDate` + balance, not stored. Flagged so nobody "fixes" it.                                                                                                                | Keep derived.                                                                                                         |
| `Order.orderNumber`, `Invoice.invoiceNumber` unique strings             | Fine. Numbering must not clash with MYOB's sequence if both systems issue numbers.                                                                                                                                    | Recommended: MYOB owns invoice numbers (Q1c, A4).                                                                     |

## 4. Suggested MVP cut (for the brief, not a design)

**Must:** G1 coffee/SKU split, G2 customer pricing, G3 standing orders, G4 delivery days + roast date, G5 kg-to-roast report, G6 frozen invoice lines + MYOB invoice ID, and G22 MYOB integration.

**Should, next:** G7 to G11, G14 to G19, G23, G27.

**Later:** G12, G13 (MYOB owns them), G20, G21, G24 to G26, G28.

All Must items assume the answers to Q1 to Q6 in `questions-for-roaster.md`. Re-check this list once those are answered.
